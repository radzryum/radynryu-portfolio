AESPA is a South Korean girl group formed by SM Entertainment. The group consists of four members: Karina, Giselle, Winter, and Ningning.

Each member has a corresponding virtual avatar, or "æ", who exists in the virtual world and interacts with the real-world members. This concept is part of SM Entertainment's larger SM Culture Universe (SMCU).

AESPA debuted in November 2020 with their single "Black Mamba" and has since gained international recognition. In 2024, they released their first full studio album Armageddon and their 5th mini album Whiplash, solidifying their status as one of K-Pop's most innovative acts.
