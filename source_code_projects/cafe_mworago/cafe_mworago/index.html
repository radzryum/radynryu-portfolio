Welcome to Café Mworago, where your favorite K-Pop music meets delicious food and drinks. Our cafe is designed to provide the ultimate K-Pop fan experience with themed decor, exclusive merchandise, and regular fan events.

Enjoy our signature bingsu while watching the latest K-Pop MVs on our big screens, or participate in our weekly dance cover competitions!
